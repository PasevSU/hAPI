## ADR-XXXX: <Short title>

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by ADR-XXXX
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Context:** What forces are at play? Why is a decision needed now?
- **Decision:** The chosen option, stated clearly.
- **Options considered:** (A) … (B) … (C) … — with trade-offs.
- **Consequences:** Positive and negative, including migration cost.
- **Validation:** How will we know this was the right call?
